### General Evaluation

**1. Learning & Working Environment:**
The program was organized very methodically and professionally. The learning roadmap was clear and detailed, making it easy for interns to track their weekly progress. The combination of self-study according to the roadmap and workshops provided maximum flexibility.

**2. Support from Mentors & Team Admin:**
The program's mentor team not only had solid expertise but was also very dedicated. Whenever I faced difficulties in configuring secure networks or debugging CI/CD processes, the mentors always guided me to trace system logs instead of providing immediate solutions; this helped me improve my problem-solving mindset. 

**3. Relevance of the Work to the Major:**
As a student majoring in Networks and Information Systems at **[Your University Name]**, I found the hands-on exercises to be highly relevant to my major. Configuring isolated VPCs, Public/Private Subnets, setting up Security Groups, Network ACLs, and applying the Principle of Least Privilege through IAM policies helped me gain a deep understanding of cloud infrastructure security.

**4. Learning Opportunities & Skill Development:**
The program provided me with an excellent opportunity to access modern cloud components such as Serverless (Lambda, API Gateway), Containers (Docker, ECS), and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation). Besides technical skills, I also improved my technical report writing and time management skills.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit:**
The AWS Study Group community was truly a great environment for learning. The knowledge-sharing atmosphere was very open; everyone was unafraid to ask questions and willing to help others, eagerly supporting and actively helping peers debug complex lab exercises.

### Survey Questions

**What made you most satisfied during the internship?**
Being provided with a real AWS sandbox account to directly practice the exercises. Instead of just learning dry theory, I was able to deploy infrastructure myself, solve arising errors independently, and learn how to manage resource costs on the Cloud.

**What do you think the program should improve for future interns?**
Add short weekly progress check-ins for study groups so interns can interact directly and share common configuration errors they encounter.

**Would you recommend this program to your friends?**
Absolutely. FCAJ is one of the few Cloud training programs with a clear, practical roadmap that is closest to the current recruitment needs of businesses.

### Suggestions & Expectations

**Suggestions:** The organizers could design additional in-depth sessions on real-world cloud security breaches to provide information security students with a practical perspective on security incident response.

**Expectations:** I very much look forward to continuing to be engaged with the AWS Study Group community in the future to stay updated on the latest cloud technology trends.
